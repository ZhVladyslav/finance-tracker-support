## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-08

First major release. The app was rebuilt from scratch: new web app, Android app and admin panel.

### ✨ Features

**Groups and money**
- Groups for one person, a couple or a family: accounts, categories, transactions and statistics in one place.
- Four transaction types — expense, income, transfer, adjustment.
- Accounts in different currencies with totals in the group's main currency.
- Expense with several items, each with its own category, quantity and price.
- Deals: money goes out and comes back in parts; the app shows profit or loss.
- Planned transactions with date and time and a reminder when they are due.
- Transaction templates — one click to a ready transaction.
- Wishlist with priorities; close a wish with an expense.
- Statistics for any period by categories and accounts.
- Group change history: who changed what and when.

**Transactions screen**
- One "Transactions" screen for all types: table on desktop, cards on phone.
- Filters by type, account, category, period, name and amount.
- An expense with several items expands right in the list.
- "Refresh" button in every list.

**Smart input**
- Receipt scan from a photo → expense with items.
- Automatic categories for items, learning from your choices.
- Import from Monobank and PrivatBank statements, CSV/Excel files and other trackers.
- Apple Pay on iPhone: a payment becomes an expense automatically.

**Together**
- Invite members by email and give them roles.
- Group chat in real time: text, files, replies, "typing…" and "read".
- Photos, videos and PDFs from chat open inside the app.
- Text formatting in notes and chat.
- Notification center, the same on all your devices.
- Push notifications for unread notifications.

**Account**
- Sign in with a password or a code from email.
- Sign in on a new device by scanning a QR code.
- Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app.
- Several accounts in one browser, switching without a password.
- Manage active sessions on all devices.
- Download all your data as an archive.
- Delete your account; you have 14 days to change your mind.

**Apps**
- Demo mode — try the app without registration.
- Welcome tour and quick setup of the first group.
- Offline mode: keep adding and editing transactions without internet, they sync when you are back online.
- Android app; the web app can be installed on your phone.
- New website in Ukrainian and English.

### 🔧 Improvements

- New modern design with better readability in light and dark themes.
- Comfortable on phone, tablet and desktop.
- Account balances update live.
- Lists update right after you save — no reload.
- Buttons show progress while an action runs.
- New date and time picker.
- Short "Created", "Saved", "Deleted" messages.
- Clear error messages in your language.
- You are marked "You" in the members list.
- Category is chosen for each item of an expense.
- Redesigned chat.

### 🛠 Bug Fixes

- You are no longer signed out when using the app in several tabs.
- Accounts and categories with transactions can no longer be deleted by mistake.
- Editing an expense no longer opens an empty form.
- Changes to a deal are saved again.
- Account balances stay correct when several changes happen at once.
- The home page opens correctly.
- Pages open offline even if you have not visited them before.
- Photos from iPhone upload correctly.
- Receipt scan, import and export work with uploaded files again.
- Notifications no longer load twice.
- A missing page shows "Not found" instead of a general error.

### ⚠️ Removed

- Category tags — a category is now just a name.
- Separate pages for expenses, incomes, transfers and adjustments — use "Transactions".
